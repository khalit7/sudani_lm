sudani_lm — sample conversation
================================================================================

Model      : checkpoints/sudani_llm_pretraining/stage_d/best.pt  (Stage D, step 90)
             110M params · 12 layers · d_model 768 · v2_32k tokenizer
Interface  : python inference.py --speaker "Abrar 8loob"
Sampling   : temperature 0.8 · top_k 50 · top_p 0.95 · max_new_tokens 48
Speaker    : "Abrar 8loob" — a real contact from the training corpus
             (2,723 conversations, the second-largest chat)

The human turns below were written by Claude, responding to whatever the model
had just said. Nothing is scripted and nothing is cherry-picked except where
explicitly marked.

================================================================================
TRANSCRIPT
================================================================================

Abrar 8loob : السلام عليكم يا خالد، كيفك؟ وينك من زمن
ME          : لا عندي مشكله انا و انتي في البيت عشان ما ممكن تجي بدري انتي و انتي في البيت ؟

Abrar 8loob : ايوا انا في البيت، انت متين بترجع؟
ME          : اي اي بس لازم تجي بدري كدا

Abrar 8loob : طيب تمام. اكلت حاجة ولا لسه؟
ME          : اي اي اي اي

Abrar 8loob : يعني شنو اي اي 😂 جاوبني بالله
ME          : و اكلت حاجه ؟

Abrar 8loob : لسه ما اكلت، عملت لينا عصيدة. تجي تاكل معانا؟
ME          : ما مشكله لكن ما عارف والله

Abrar 8loob : خلاص يا زول، انا حستناك. لا تتأخر
ME          : انا اسف

================================================================================
WHAT WORKS
================================================================================

* Correct dialect, not MSA. Sudanese particles and constructions throughout:
  "ما عارف والله", "بس لازم تجي بدري كدا", "ما مشكله", "كدا".

* Gendered agreement learned from the real chat. It addresses Abrar with the
  feminine "انتي", never the masculine "انت" — nobody told it who Abrar is; it
  inferred that from 2,723 conversations with her.

* Chat register, not prose. Replies are 2-13 words, lowercase-equivalent,
  no punctuation ceremony. That is what the corpus actually looks like.

* Multi-turn context is carried. Turn 4's "و اكلت حاجه ؟" echoes the food topic
  Abrar raised in turn 3.

================================================================================
WHAT DOES NOT
================================================================================

* It does not track who holds which fact. Turn 1 answers "how are you, where
  have you been" with a question about *Abrar* coming home early — the roles are
  swapped. Turn 4 asks "and did you eat?" immediately after Abrar asked exactly
  that. It has learned the shape of the exchange, not its state.

* Degeneration into "اي اي اي…". Turn 3 shows a mild case. On a different
  sampling seed, turn 4 produced 48 consecutive "اي" tokens, hitting the length
  cap — that sample is omitted from the transcript above but is the honest
  failure mode, and it appears in roughly 1 reply in 20.

* Replies are reactive, never initiating. It answers; it does not ask, plan, or
  carry a thread forward on its own.

* "انا اسف" closing an invitation to dinner is fluent and contextually wrong.

================================================================================
CALIBRATION
================================================================================

This is a 110M-parameter model trained from scratch on 5B tokens of Arabic and
~7.9M tokens of chat, on two consumer GPUs, in under five hours total. It is
roughly 1/1500th the size of a frontier model.

Judged as "does it produce Sudanese chat" — yes, convincingly.
Judged as "does it understand the conversation" — no, and it should not be
expected to at this scale i think.

Measured: owner-reply perplexity 181.4, held-out chat perplexity 74.3,
Flores Sudanese perplexity 343.9. See plan.md for the full comparison against
Stage C, which is better on generality but 12% worse at reproducing the owner's
own replies.
